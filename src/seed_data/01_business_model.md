# CTI Soluciones: B2B Business Model

CTI Soluciones specializes in the design and development of **Enterprise Intranets and Custom Corporate Portals**, integrating powerful Artificial Intelligence tools and **Deep Search / RAG** engines over companies' internal documentation.

## Value Proposition
1. **Reduction of Operational Time:** Employees find policies, manuals, and SOPs in seconds thanks to the conversational AI engine.
2. **Corporate Security and Control:** Robust role-based architecture (RBAC) and enterprise authentication (SSO / Azure AD / Google Workspace).
3. **Scalability and Performance:** Built on a modern and efficient tech stack (Next.js + Supabase + pgvector).
