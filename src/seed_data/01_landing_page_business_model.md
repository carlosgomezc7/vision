# Business Model: High-Impact Custom Landing Pages
*CTI Soluciones — Carlos Gómez*

The primary business objective is delivering bespoke, beautiful, accessible, and high-converting Landing Pages for corporate and commercial clients.

Key Value Drivers:
1. Speed to Market: Ready to deploy in days using modular Next.js components.
2. Tailored to the Client's Taste: No generic cookie-cutter templates. Every landing page reflects the client's colors, typography, aesthetic references, and tone.
3. Conversion Focus: Clear primary call-to-action (WhatsApp direct, lead capture forms, call-to-action buttons).
4. Accessibility & Quality: Built on strict WCAG 2.2 AA standards and modern Core Web Vitals performance benchmarks.
