# Hero Section and CTA Blueprint

The Hero section is the make-or-break element of a landing page. It must answer three questions in under 5 seconds:

1. What do you do? (Clear, benefit-oriented H1 headline).
2. Who is it for? (Concise subheadline explaining the value proposition).
3. What should I do next? (Irresistible Primary Call to Action button with high contrast).

Visual Rules:
- Prominent CTA button with minimum 4.5:1 contrast against the background.
- Floating WhatsApp badge or header phone button for immediate trust and action.
- Visual anchor: client product imagery, mockup, or clean illustrative geometry.
