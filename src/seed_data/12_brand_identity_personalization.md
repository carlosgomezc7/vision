# Brand Identity and Visual Personalization

Personalizing a landing page to the client's wishes requires mapping aesthetic adjectives to design tokens:

- "Minimalist & Modern": Generous white space, high-contrast monochrome palette with a vibrant accent color, clean sans-serif typography (`Inter`, `Geist`).
- "Warm & Artisanal": Earthy tones (terracotta, forest green, warm cream), serif or humanistic fonts (`Playfair`, `Merriweather`), rounded cards.
- "Corporate & Executive": Deep navy blues, slate grays, crisp geometry, clean sans-serif (`Roboto`, `Montserrat`).
- "Bold & High-Tech": Dark mode backgrounds (`#0F172A`), electric blue or cyan accents, subtle glow borders, monospaced accents.
