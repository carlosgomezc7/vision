# Conversion Copywriting and Section Structure

A compelling landing page guides the reader through an emotional and logical narrative:

1. Hero: Attention grabber, core value proposition, primary CTA.
2. Problem & Agitation: Acknowledging what the client's customers struggle with.
3. Solution & Benefits: How the client solves the problem uniquely.
4. Social Proof / Testimonials: Real client outcomes, ratings, or logos.
5. Pricing or Offer Summary: Transparent expectations or free quote proposal.
6. FAQ: Answering objections before they are asked.
7. Final CTA & Contact: Direct contact form, phone click, and WhatsApp link.
