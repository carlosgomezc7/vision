# Client Listening and Intake Methodology

Effective client intake starts with deep, empathetic listening. Carlos Gómez prioritizes listening to the client's vision and business story without technical jargon.

Key Discovery Areas:
1. Brand Essence: What makes the client's company distinct and trusted?
2. Conversion Goal: What is the primary action a visitor should take? (WhatsApp, Phone call, Contact form, Schedule meeting).
3. Visual Taste & References: What websites does the client love? What adjectives define their dream aesthetic? (Modern, minimalist, elegant, warm, bold).
4. Brand Palette & Constraints: Corporate hex colors and disliked colors.
5. Sections: The essential blocks the client wants on their page.
